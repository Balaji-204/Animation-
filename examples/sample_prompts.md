# OfflineAnimStudio Sample Story Prompts

Here are tested, high-yield prompts designed for the offline 3D animation pipeline:

### 1. The Desert Bloom (Low-Poly Cartoon)
> "A small spherical scout robot wanders across orange sand dunes during sunset. Its antenna twitches as it discovers a single glowing magenta flower growing out of the sand. The robot crouches down, extends its metallic hand, and gently shades it from the wind."

### 2. Deep Space Janitor (Low-Poly Sci-Fi)
> "An orbital cleaning drone is floating outside a massive space station. It accidentally bumps into a mysterious floating shipping crate. The crate unlocks, revealing an ancient digital memory crystal."

### 3. The Forest Guardian (Stylized Cel-Shaded)
> "A miniature wooden automaton awakens in a mossy ruin covered in ivy. It turns around to greet an curious glowing firefly that hovers in front of its optical sensors."

### 4. Low-VRAM Fast Test (1 Scene, Fast Render)
> "A scout robot waves hello directly to the camera on a desert dune during golden hour."
